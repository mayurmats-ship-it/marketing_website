How to judge a LEGO discount

A discount is only useful when it is measured against a credible price. A listing that says “40% off” may still be expensive if the starting price is inflated.

Match the exact set number

Compare the same LEGO set number across stores. Similar names and packaging can conceal different editions, piece counts or product generations.

Compare the final checkout price

Include delivery fees, coupons and payment conditions. Prefer a documented recent selling price to a seller's claimed original price.

Check who is selling

Read seller details, returns policy and packaging descriptions. A steep discount is less attractive if the product is damaged, incomplete or difficult to return.

Ask whether you want the set

For collectors, storage space and box condition matter. For builders, the experience of assembling and displaying a set may matter more than resale prospects. A deal is not a saving if you would not otherwise buy it.
