COLLECTING

Should you keep LEGO sets sealed?

Keeping a set sealed preserves its unopened condition, but does not guarantee that it will increase in value.

Why some collectors keep boxes unopened

Collectors may prefer original seals and undamaged packaging. Discontinued products can become harder to find, but demand varies widely between themes and sets.

Costs that are easy to overlook

Storage, humidity, sunlight, shipping damage, marketplace fees and the time required to find a buyer can reduce any eventual profit.

What to evaluate before buying

Consider the set's appeal, availability, purchase price and your own interest. Do not assume retirement automatically means appreciation. Past resale prices are not a reliable forecast.

Our rule of thumb

Only buy a collectible you would be comfortable owning even if it never becomes more valuable. If you love building, opening the box may be the better return.
